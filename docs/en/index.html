G2 Boost: Windows gaming performance, no tricks.

A desktop app that brings together what usually takes several tools: hardware diagnostics, cleanup, debloat, networking, games and performance tweaks. All with preview, backup and one-click undo.

v0.9.3Windows 10/11 · x64 · PT-BR & EN

Released Oct 28, 2026

  • 62 reversible performance tweaks, profiles and one-click undo
  • Injection-free in-game overlay (FPS and 1% low) and a “Prepare & play” game library
  • PC check-up with ~20 measurements, sensor tree and CSV export
  • 3-level debloat, cleanup with disk treemap and winget app installer
  • Network tools: bufferbloat, MTR and DNS; NVIDIA and AMD GPU settings

Before data, I built software: at PGFN (Brazil’s National Treasury Attorney’s Office) I shipped a digital repository platform in 8 months, saving R$ 1 million. Today I combine both worlds: pipelines, dimensional modeling and Power BI on one side; Angular, Python, Java and desktop apps on the other.
